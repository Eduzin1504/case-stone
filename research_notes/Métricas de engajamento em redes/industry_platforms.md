# How the industry and the platforms define and weight engagement: Instagram and YouTube (2023–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-23. Many findings came through search-engine summaries of pages. Where I fetched the page itself, I say so. Items marked [UNVERIFIED] come from secondary sources only, or from background knowledge I did not re-check against a primary source.

## Q1. Engagement-rate formulas, who uses them, pros and cons, and benchmarks by follower tier

### Takeaway
Every ER formula has the same numerator: interactions, usually likes + comments, and sometimes shares and saves too. They differ only in the denominator: followers, reach, impressions or views. Tools that benchmark competitors (Rival IQ, Socialinsider's competitive mode) divide by followers, because reach, saves and shares are private to the account owner. Owned-account analytics prefer reach. Benchmarks differ by an order of magnitude depending on the vendor's formula. Rival IQ puts the Instagram median at 0.36% by followers. Socialinsider puts it at about 3% by reach. Buffer reports a 4.3% median. So never compare numbers across vendors without matching the formula.

### Cited Findings

**Formula catalogue (Hootsuite, "How to calculate engagement rate in 2026", Oct 6, 2026, fetched)** — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/calculate-engagement-rate/)
- **ER by reach (ERR)** = engagements on post / reach of post × 100, then averaged across posts. Pro: measures the people who actually saw the post, including non-followers. Con: reach swings a lot, and very low reach inflates the rate.
- **ER by post (by followers)** = engagements / total followers × 100. Pro: stable when reach fluctuates. Con: ignores viral reach beyond followers, and it falls as follower count grows.
- **ER by impressions** = engagements / impressions × 100. Pro: suits paid and CPM campaigns. Con: gives lower rates because repeat views count, and impressions can be inconsistent.
- **Daily ER** = engagements in a day / followers × 100. Pro: captures engagement on older posts. Con: cannot tell one superfan from many fans, and it depends on how much the account posts.
- **ER by views** = engagements on a video / video views × 100. Con: repeat views from the same user lower the rate.
- **Cost per engagement** = spend / engagements.

**Socialinsider**
- ER by followers (Instagram) = (likes + comments) / followers × 100. ER by reach = (likes + comments) / post reach × 100 — [Socialinsider formula guide](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/engagement-rate-formula/)
- Socialinsider excludes saves because "Instagram API doesn't offer access to the saves metric for competitors' profiles". It recommends the follower-based formula for competitor comparisons because "reach is a private metric and an estimated one" — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/engagement-rate-formula/)
- 2025 benchmarks:
  - By reach: Instagram averaged 3.00% ER by reach in 2025, from a May 2025 study of posts from May 2024 to May 2025 — [Socialinsider reach study](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/)
  - Per post by format (data from January 2023 to December 2024): carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.50%, images 0.45% — [Socialinsider Instagram Benchmarks 2025 PDF](https://www.socialinsider.io/data-geeks/instagram_benchmarks_2025.pdf)
  - These figures came from a search summary; I did not open the PDF.

**Rival IQ (2025 Social Media Industry Benchmark Report, published Feb 25, 2025, fetched)**
- Engagement counts likes, comments, favorites, retweets, shares and reactions. "Engagement rate is calculated based on all these interactions divided by total follower count." — [Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/)
- Sample: 150 companies per industry across 14 industries, drawn from a database of more than 200,000. Selected companies had 25K–1M Facebook fans and at least 1K Instagram and Twitter followers. The data covers more than 4M posts and 9B interactions, and the report uses medians — [Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/)
- Year-over-year ER changes: Instagram −16%, Facebook −36%, TikTok −34% — [Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/)
- All-industry Instagram median ER per post: 0.36% (2025 report) — [Rival IQ "good engagement rate on Instagram"](https://www.rivaliq.com/?p=30050), via a search summary.
- Dash Social's 2025 report gives a similar figure, an overall Instagram average of 0.3% — [Dash Social H2 2025](https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2025/H2/instagram.pdf)

**Buffer (Sep 2, 2025, fetched): 27M+ posts from 273K accounts, overall median ER 4.3%** — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-engagement-rate/)

  - The 2026 report reportedly gives nano 1.78% and micro (10K–50K) 0.54% — [search summary, HypeAuditor benchmarking category](https://hypeauditor.com/blog/category/influencer-industry/benchmarking)

**Influencer Marketing Hub / "true reach"**

- Practitioner guides describe reach-based ER as the one that suits non-follower campaigns, with a worked example: 300 engagements / 6,000 reach = 5% — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/blog/influencer-engagement-rate); [Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/how-to-calculate-influencer-engagement-rate)
- IMH survey figures quoted by third parties conflict: 78%, 87% and 73% of brands are each said to prioritise ER, so do not cite these without the original report — search summary.

### Inferences
- Benchmarks split into two clusters:
  - Follower-based brand medians (Rival IQ and Dash Social): about 0.3–0.5%.
  - Reach-based or creator-heavy samples (Socialinsider by reach, Buffer): about 3–5%.
- The gap between the clusters is mostly the denominator, not performance.
- Follower-based ER falls as tier size rises, which is the classic small-account inflation. Buffer's data shows the curve flattening and even reversing at 1M+, which fits Reels reach no longer tracking follower count.
- For a study built on public scraped data, the only formulas you can compute for third-party accounts are (likes + comments) / followers and (likes + comments) / views (Reels) — see Q4.

## Q2. What Instagram officially says it ranks on, its Insights metric definitions, and originality

### Takeaway
Since January 2025, Mosseri has named watch time, likes and sends (DM shares) as the top three ranking signals, and he told creators to watch average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Likes weigh more for content from accounts a viewer follows. Sends weigh more for content shown to non-followers. Comments and saves were not among the three. In August 2024 Instagram made "Views" the primary metric across formats, and it later dropped Impressions and Plays from Insights and the API. Originality ranking dates to 2022 and was tightened against repost accounts in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri, quoted Jan 22, 2025 (fetched): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends. So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." Also: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." Comments were not mentioned — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- Earlier Q&A, reported around early 2024 [exact date UNVERIFIED]: Mosseri called sends per reach "one of the most important signals we use in ranking" and said it correlates with reach more than anything else. One report paraphrases this as "share rate" — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-says-post-share-rates-are-now-a-key-driver-of-reach/716540/); [SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389)
- August 2024: Mosseri announced that Views would become the primary metric for Reels, Stories, photos and carousels, with "Plays" relabelled as Views. He said "Sends, reach, and views are the most important metrics for anybody trying to understand how their content is doing" — [Tubefilter Aug 7, 2024](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/08/07/instagram-views-reels-posts-multi-format-adam-mosseri/amp/); [PetaPixel Aug 8, 2024](https://petapixel.com/2024/08/08/reels-stories-carousels-likes-followers-instagram-will-now-focus-on-views-in-metrics-for-photos); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-metrics-to-focus-creators-on-views/723645/)
- Impressions were removed from Insights but stay in Meta Ads Manager. Sources disagree on the dates:
  - One says Meta announced the API change on a developer blog on Jan 8, 2025, with 90 days' notice.
  - One says impressions were gone from profiles and creator accounts by April 2025.
  - One claims "since April 2024" in the API, which looks wrong.

  Sources: [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/instagram-views-metrics-changes); [Metricool](https://help.metricool.com/en/article/instagram-replaces-impressions-with-views-what-you-need-to-know-ustr2f); [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/en-us/blog/views-are-the-new-impressions-what-metas-metrics-shift-means-for-influencer-marketing). My recollection is that the Graph API v22 deprecated `impressions`, `plays` and `video_views` around April 21, 2025 [UNVERIFIED].
- Meta developer docs (fetched):
  - **reach** = "number of unique accounts that have seen the media object."
  - **impressions** = total times seen, including repeat views.
  - **engagement** = likes + comments.
  - Media insights need owner permissions (`instagram_business_manage_insights` or `instagram_manage_insights`) and cover only media owned by professional accounts.

  Source: [Meta Instagram Platform Insights](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/insights)
- Originality:
  - Apr 20, 2022: Mosseri said ranking would "value original content more", especially compared with reposts, and that aggregators would be less likely to be recommended. He admitted classifiers only predict originality: "that's not knowing" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/20/instagram-ranking-system-recommend-original-content/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)
  - Apr 30, 2026: an update targets accounts built on reposts across Reels, photos and carousels. Mostly-repost accounts become effectively invisible in Explore and other recommendation surfaces. Instagram reportedly swaps reposted Reels for the original clip in recommendations and labels the original creator — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/). I did not fetch this page. The Engadget date for the "replace with original" detail is unconfirmed; it may be the 2024 Mosseri change [UNVERIFIED].

### Inferences
- Instagram's own guidance is reach-normalised ("likes per reach", "sends per reach"). That is effectively ERR by component, not follower-based ER.
- Because sends matter more for unconnected (non-follower) distribution, dividing by followers is misaligned with how Reels are actually distributed.
- Two of the three named signals, watch time and sends, are invisible to outside observers.

### Gaps
- I could not reach the original Mosseri videos or Threads posts; the quotes come from Social Media Today.
- I could not retrieve the official Instagram Help Center text defining shares, saves and views for Insights.
- I have not confirmed the exact API deprecation date.

## Q3. YouTube and Shorts: how views are defined, engaged views, and what the Data API exposes

### Takeaway
From Mar 31, 2025, a Shorts view counts every time a Short starts or replays, with no minimum watch time. The old thresholded count lives on as "engaged views" in Advanced Mode, and engaged views still drive YPP monetisation. Reports say this "count from first frame" logic was extended to all formats in 2026. The public Data API exposes viewCount, likeCount and commentCount. dislikeCount has been owner-only since Dec 13, 2021. There is no public shares or watch-time field.

### Cited Findings
- Effective Mar 31, 2025 (article dated Mar 26, 2025, fetched):
  - A Shorts view counts each time a Short "starts to play or replay". Previously, a view needed a certain number of seconds watched.
  - The original metric was renamed "engaged views" and sits in YouTube Analytics Advanced Mode.
  - YPP eligibility and earnings stay based on engaged views.

  Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/26/youtube-shorts-views-counting-stats/)
- Engaged views explainer — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/) (not fetched)
- YouTube Help, "How engagement metrics are counted" (fetched Nov 2026):
  - Views are now counted "the moment a video starts to play across all formats."
  - From November 2026, public watch-page counts add co-views estimated for TV viewing.
  - YPP earnings use engaged views and engaged watch hours.
  - Likes and dislikes are listed as engagement metrics.
  - Low-quality playbacks may be discarded, and counts may be frozen or adjusted.

  Source: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/2991785?hl=en)
- A search summary says counting from the first frame across Shorts, long-form, podcasts and live started Aug 24, 2026 — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-views-on-long-form-live/586166/); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-view-count-update/) [secondary, date not verified on a primary page].
- YouTube Data API v3 `videos.statistics` (fetched):
  - `viewCount`: counted when playback begins, including autoplay and hover.
  - `likeCount` and `commentCount`.
  - `dislikeCount`: "included in an API response only if the API request was authenticated by the video owner", effective Dec 13, 2021.
  - `favoriteCount`: deprecated in 2015 and always 0.
  - No shares or watch-time fields.

  Source: [YouTube Data API videos resource](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos)
- Watch time, average view duration, retention, shares, subscribers gained and engaged views are available only to the owner through YouTube Analytics and the YouTube Analytics API [background knowledge, consistent with the Data API doc above; UNVERIFIED in this session].

### Inferences
- Since March 2025, (likes + comments) / views for Shorts uses a much larger, replay-inclusive denominator than before. ER by views computed from public Shorts data is therefore deflated relative to 2024 and earlier, so a longitudinal series breaks at Mar 31, 2025, and for long-form at about Aug 2026.
- Public `viewCount` is not the metric YouTube pays on (engaged views).

### Gaps
- I did not fetch YouTube's own announcement post for the March 2025 Shorts change or the 2026 all-format change.
- I found no official statement of the engaged-view threshold in seconds.

## Q4. What public scraping can get (the Apify instagram-scraper)

### Takeaway
The official `apify/instagram-scraper` returns `likesCount`, `commentsCount`, `videoViewCount`, `videoPlayCount` and `videoDuration` per post. Follower counts come from the profile output. It returns no saves, shares, sends, reach or watch time, which are exactly the signals Instagram says matter most. So public ER is limited to likes and comments over followers or views.

### Cited Findings
- Output fields for `apify/instagram-scraper` (actor docs, fetched) — [Apify Instagram Scraper](https://apify.com/apify/instagram-scraper.md):
  - Identity: `id`, `shortCode`, `url`, `type`, `productType`
  - Content: `caption`, `hashtags`, `mentions`
  - Engagement: `likesCount`, `commentsCount`, `videoViewCount`, `videoPlayCount`, `latestComments`, `isCommentsDisabled`
  - Video and audio: `videoUrl`, `videoDuration`, `musicInfo`
  - Timing and flags: `timestamp`, `isPinned`
  - Owner: `ownerUsername`, `ownerId`
  - `followersCount` appears only in the profile details output.
  - Saves, shares and reach do not appear.
- Count caveats from the same docs:
  - Hidden likes come back as `likesCount = -1`.
  - The actor sees the logged-out page, so counts may be lower than what a logged-in user sees.
  - Values are snapshots taken at run time.
- The docs do not define the difference between `videoViewCount` and `videoPlayCount`. Users have asked in an [Apify issue](https://apify.com/apify/instagram-post-scraper/issues/video-play-counts-mi-Z9MLa1rezMru0ltev) with no clear answer.
- Reported bugs:
  - `videoPlayCount` always 0, and `likesCount` -1 on some reels (closed issue) — [Reel Scraper issue](https://apify.com/apify/instagram-reel-scraper/issues/videoplaycount--0-li-JW5XPst0hWkYESA70)
  - `videoViewCount` showed 1,866 while the app showed 5,533 — [Profile Scraper issue](https://apify.com/apify/instagram-profile-scraper/issues/videoviewcount-withi-JX8kehwUle1569npZ)
- A third-party actor says `videoViewCount` is populated only when Instagram exposes it — [topapi actor](https://apify.com/topapi/instagram-scraper.md)
- The likely mapping, by inference: `videoPlayCount` is the public "views"/plays number shown on Reels after the 2024 relabel, and `videoViewCount` is the legacy video-view count [UNVERIFIED].
- The Instagram Graph API Business Discovery endpoint lets a business account read another professional account's `followers_count`, `media_count`, and per-media `like_count` and `comments_count`. It gives no insights such as reach, saves or shares [background knowledge, UNVERIFIED in this session].

### Inferences
- For third-party Instagram accounts, the computable formulas are:
  - ER_followers = (likes + comments) / followers
  - ER_views (Reels only) = (likes + comments) / `videoPlayCount`
- Neither formula captures sends or saves. Accounts with hidden likes (-1) must be excluded or handled with comments only.
- Run a small validation against the in-app view counts before trusting `videoViewCount` or `videoPlayCount`.

### Gaps
- There is no official Apify definition of `videoViewCount` vs `videoPlayCount`.
- I did not check whether the separate `apify/instagram-reel-scraper` really exposes share counts; its docs only mention shares.

## Q5. Practitioner critiques of follower-based ER

### Takeaway
Follower-based ER systematically favours small accounts, can be distorted by fake or inactive followers in either direction, and does not reflect Reels distribution, which reaches mostly non-followers. Practitioners recommend reach- or views-based ER, or comparing only within a tier.

### Cited Findings
- Follower-based ER ignores viral reach beyond followers and tends to drop as follower count grows. ERR captures non-followers but gets inflated when reach is low — [Hootsuite 2026](https://blog.hootsuite.com/calculate-engagement-rate/)
- Small accounts show higher rates because their audiences are "tight-knit" (friends, early supporters). Buffer advises comparing only within the same tier — [Buffer 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-engagement-rate/)
- Fake or mass followers make ER unusually low because those accounts are inactive. A large gap between follower-based and impression-based ER can signal fake followers or limited organic reach — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/blog/influencer-engagement-rate); [Influencer Hero](https://www.influencer-hero.com/blogs/fake-follower-check-metrics-tools-to-analyze-fake-followers)
- Reels distribution: Mosseri distinguishes connected content (from accounts a viewer follows) from unconnected content (recommended to non-followers), and says sends matter more for unconnected content. That implies a large share of Reels reach is non-follower distribution — [Social Media Today Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/). I found no official Meta percentage for the non-follower share of Reels reach.
- Reach is itself "an estimated" private metric, which is why Socialinsider still uses followers for competitor work — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/engagement-rate-formula/)
- HypeAuditor reportedly switched to a views denominator for Reels-heavy accounts, which signals that the industry is moving away from followers [UNVERIFIED secondary] — [Colorlib](https://colorlib.com/wp/instagram-engagement-rate/)

### Inferences
- When follower-based ER is computed from public data, report it alongside the views-based version and stratify by tier.
- Treat engagement pods or comment bots as an inflating factor: comment counts are public but not quality-weighted. I found no primary source on pods in this session.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative statistic on the share of Reels reach that comes from non-followers.
- I found no primary academic or vendor study quantifying fake-follower distortion of ER in 2023–2026.
